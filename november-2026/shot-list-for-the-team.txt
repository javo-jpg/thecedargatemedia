SHOT LIST FOR THE TEAM · Phone clips and photos, November 2026

WHY WE NEED THIS
Right now every Reel comes from the same promo shoot: the same few people, the same rooms. People notice. Fresh clips from real days at The Cedar Gate (real shooters, real guests, real staff) are what make people stop scrolling and follow. A phone is all you need.

HOW TO FILM (takes 10 minutes per event)
- Hold the phone vertical (up and down), not sideways.
- Film 5 to 10 seconds per clip. Hold still and let the moment happen. No zooming.
- Shoot with the sun behind you or to the side. Morning and late afternoon light look best.
- Wipe the lens first.
- Normal video is fine. If your phone has "Cinematic" or 4K, even better. Please don't add filters.
- Ask people "Is it OK if we post this?" Anyone who says no stays out of the shot.

AT EVERY CHARITY SHOOT (Nov 6, 7, 12, 13, 16, 20)
1. Arrival: trucks pulling in, people checking in, the welcome table.
2. Squads walking out to the course, from behind.
3. The first pull: a shooter calls "pull" and the clay breaks (film from the side, 10 to 12 feet back, with ear protection).
4. Close-ups: shells being loaded, a hand on the gun, the scorecard, a golf cart rolling.
5. Reactions: high fives, laughing, a squad celebrating a good station.
6. One photo of every squad together (horizontal is fine for this one).
7. Lunch: the food being served and the full table.
8. The winners and sponsors: the check or the trophy, and the sponsor banner.
Send clips the same day to the "Raw footage (drop files here)" folder in Drive, or text them to Javier.

AROUND THE PROPERTY (any day, whenever someone has 5 minutes)
- Staff at work: the chef plating, someone setting the long table, the range crew loading a trap. Get names and an OK.
- Guests in the cabins or on the porch in the morning (with their OK).
- The dogs.
- The pheasant tower and a group hunt.
- Holiday decorations going up in the lodge and the barns (late November). We'll use these for December.
- Sunrise and sunset over the ponds, from the same spot each time.

WHAT WE DO WITH IT
- Nov 16 Reel "On the course this month", built only from new clips.
- Every recap post (thank-you carousels after each shoot).
- December content: holiday parties, the members' Christmas party, gift ideas.
